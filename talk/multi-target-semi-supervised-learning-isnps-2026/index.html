Multi-target semi-supervised learning with application to small area estimation

Katarzyna Reluga

Abstract

This invited talk extends semi-supervised learning to settings with several quantities to estimate across subpopulations that have few labelled observations. It discusses estimators designed to improve stability and efficiency, and shows how small area estimation fits into this framework.
